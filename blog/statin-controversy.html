A Better Conversation About Statins

A useful treatment discussion includes both the expected benefit and a plan for symptoms.

Updated September 8, 2026

Start here

A useful treatment discussion includes both the expected benefit and a plan for symptoms.

Ask what the medicine will do for you

When a statin is recommended, ask why it fits your situation. A previous heart attack, substantial plaque, diabetes or other risk factors can change the expected benefit. Your treatment should have a goal you understand.

Make room for concerns

If you develop symptoms, report them. The next step is to work out what is happening and find a treatment you can tolerate. A change in dose or medicine may help; sometimes another cause needs attention.

SAMSON showed that taking a tablet and expectations about treatment can contribute to symptom burden. It did not show that every patient's symptoms are unrelated to a statin. A careful, respectful review is more useful than dismissing concerns.

The statin guide explains the study and the options to discuss with your clinician.

Questions for your next visit

  • What benefit should I expect from treatment?
  • What will we do if I develop symptoms?
